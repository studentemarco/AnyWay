PROJECT DESCRIPTION

AnyWay is a platform that allows you to navigate inside structures through maps shared by users. Designed for places such as schools, shopping centers, or large structures, AnyWay makes orientation simple and effective even where classic mapping systems do not reach. Anyone can explore the maps uploaded by the community through an intuitive interface, while those who wish to contribute with their own maps have a dedicated section to manage and publish them. The creation of the maps takes place through an external application, designed specifically for this purpose. Simply upload an image of the plan, position the points of interest, connect them with paths and the map will be ready to be integrated into the system. The internal algorithm calculates the optimal route between two points. AnyWay is also designed to be inclusive: for example marking paths inaccessible for wheelchairs or strollers and suggesting alternative routes automatically.
